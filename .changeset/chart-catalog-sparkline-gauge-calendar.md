Chart catalog per decision #29: `Sparkline` (plain-SVG trend, `aria-hidden` with a visually-hidden summary, `tone`/`palette` props, now used internally by `NumberCard`), `CalendarHeatmapChart` (echarts heatmap on the calendar coordinate system, blue or amber token ramp, cross-aware `onPointClick`), and `GaugeChart` (house-style dial: 1px track, single blue progress arc, metric typography centered, palette locked to tokens). Radar and map stay out of scope.
